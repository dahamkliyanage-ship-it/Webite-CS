Topic 04

Materials Across Engineering

No matter the specific discipline, all engineers will eventually face a design problem involving materials. A basic and applied knowledge of materials is universally required across the field .

  • Materials Engineers: Specialists who develop, process, and test materials used to create products ranging from computer chips to aircraft wings. They study the atomic structures of metals, ceramics, plastics, composites, and nanomaterials to create new substances that meet precise mechanical, electrical, and chemical requirements.
  • Civil Engineers: Design, build, and maintain public and private infrastructure, requiring an intimate knowledge of bulk structural materials like concrete and steel.
  • Mechanical Engineers: Design, build, and test mechanical and thermal sensors, engines, and machines.
  • Electrical & Computer Engineers: Design electronic equipment, power generation systems, processors, and circuit boards, relying heavily on conductive and semiconductive materials.
  • Biomedical Engineers: Combine engineering principles with medical sciences to design healthcare equipment and biocompatible devices.
  • Textile Engineers: Design the processes and equipment to create fibers, yarns, and fabrics—textiles so advanced they are even used as the lining of space shuttle cargo holds.
