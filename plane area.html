Properties of Plane Areas

ME 1033 - Properties of Plane Areas - Summary

1. Centroid & Center of Gravity

  • Centroid (C): Geometric center of a plane area where the total area is considered to be concentrated.
  • Coordinates:
x = ∫(x · dA) / A
y = ∫(y · dA) / A
  • Symmetry: If an area has an axis of symmetry, the centroid lies along it. With multiple axes, it lies at their intersection.

2. Moments of Area

  • First Moment: Evaluates distribution relative to an axis (used to locate centroid).
  • Second Moment (Moment of Inertia - I): Quantifies resistance to bending and deflection.
I_xx = ∫ y² dA | I_yy = ∫ x² dA
Polar Moment: I_zz = J_o = I_xx + I_yy
  • Standard Sections:
    • Rectangle (Centroidal): I_xx = (b · d³) / 12
    • Triangle (Base): I_base = (b · h³) / 12
    • Circle: I_xx = (π · R⁴) / 4

3. Parallel & Perpendicular Axis Theorems

  • Parallel Axis Theorem: Relates moment of inertia about any axis to a parallel centroidal axis.
I_xx = I_centroid + A · (ȳ)²
  • Perpendicular Axis Theorem: Valid for planar figures (I_zz = I_xx + I_yy).
  • Radius of Gyration: Distance where total area yields the same moment: r_x = √(I_xx / A).

4. Engineering Application & Beam Geometry

Cross-sectional shape controls structural performance under bending loads:

  • Bending Stress: σ = (M · y) / I (Larger I results in lower stress).
  • Deflection: Inversely proportional to flexural rigidity (E · I).
  • Orientation: Placing beams vertically maximizes depth (d), drastically raising I via d³ to resist bending.

5. Transformation Law (Inclined Axes)

Calculates the second moment of area when reference axes are rotated by an angle θ:

Ix1x1 = ( (Ixx + Iyy) / 2 ) + ( (Ixx - Iyy) / 2 ) · cos 2θ - Ixy · sin 2θ
Iy1y1 = ( (Ixx + Iyy) / 2 ) - ( (Ixx - Iyy) / 2 ) · cos 2θ + Ixy · sin 2θ
Ix1y1 = ( (Ixx - Iyy) / 2 ) · sin 2θ + Ixy · cos 2θ
  • Invariant Sum: Total second moment remains constant under any rotation:
Ixx + Iyy = Ix1x1 + Iy1y1 = Izz

6. Principal Axes & Properties

  • Definition: Axes about which product moment of area vanishes (Ix1y1 = 0).
  • Orientation Formula:
tan 2θ = -2 · Ixy / (Ixx - Iyy)
  • Key Characteristics:
    • Two principal axes are strictly perpendicular to each other (θ and θ + 90°).
    • Yields the maximum and minimum possible second moments of area.
    • Any axis of symmetry is automatically a principal axis.
    • If a plane area has more than 2 axes of symmetry (e.g., circle, square), every axis is a principal axis with identical I.

  • Composite Standard Sections (Centroidal Ixx):
    • Hollow Circular Tube: Ixx = (π / 64) · (D4 - d4)
    • Hollow Box: Ixx = (B · D3 - b · d3) / 12
    • Symmetric I-Beam: Ixx = (B · D3 / 12) - 2 · [b · d3 / 12]
Quick Test: Plane Areas

If a symmetrical I-section has an area where Ixx = 4000 cm4 and Iyy = 1000 cm4, what is the product moment of area Ixy, and what is the value of (Ix1x1 + Iy1y1) when axes rotate by 30°?

Show Answer & Working

1. Since the section has axes of symmetry, Ixy = 0.

2. By Invariant Sum Theorem: Ix1x1 + Iy1y1 = Ixx + Iyy = 4000 + 1000 = 5000 cm4.
