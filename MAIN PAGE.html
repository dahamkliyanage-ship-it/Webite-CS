CE1023: Fluid Mechanics

Chapter 01

Introduction & Fluid Properties

Introduction to fluid mechanics, covering fundamental dimensions, units, continuum concept, and properties like density and viscosity.
Chapter 02

Hydrostatic Pressure

Introduction to fluid statics, pressure at a point, Pascal's law, and pressure measurement using manometers and mechanical gauges.
Chapter 03

Hydrostatic Thrust

Calculation of hydrostatic forces, magnitude, direction, and lines of action (centers of pressure) on submerged flat and curved surfaces.
Chapter 04

Buoyancy & Stability

Archimedes' principle, buoyant forces, metacentric height, and stability conditions for fully submerged and floating bodies.
Chapter 05

Relative Equilibrium

Behavior and pressure distribution of fluids subjected to uniform linear acceleration and constant angular rotation (Forced Vortex Motion).
Chapter 06

Fluids in Motion

Kinematic principles of fluid in motion, classification of flows, spatial/temporal variations, and flow visualization patterns.
Chapter 07

Analysis of Fluid Flow

Applying the fundamental laws of mechanics—Conservation of Mass, Energy, and Momentum—using Control Volume analysis.
Chapter 08

Fluid Machinery

Comprehensive overview of devices that convert fluid energy to mechanical energy or vice versa, focusing on pumps and turbines.
