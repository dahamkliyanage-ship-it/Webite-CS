Engineering Mechanics

ME 1033 | 2.0 Credits | Department of Mechanical and Civil Engineering

This module introduces fundamental principles of mechanics applied to engineering problems. It covers equilibrium of rigid bodies, internal forces in structures, friction, and the kinetics and kinematics of particles and rigid systems.

Module Aim:
To develop a strong foundation in the principles of mechanics, enabling students to analyze and solve real- world engineering problems with critical thinking, using the fundamentals of statics and dynamics

Statics

Analysis of bodies at rest or moving at constant velocity. Focuses on forces, moments, and equilibrium.

  • LO-1: Calculate sectional properties of plane areas.
  • LO-2: Calculate internal forces in beams.
  • LO-3: Identify statically determinate/indeterminate trusses, their stability and determine forces in truss members.
Go to Statics Roadmap →

Dynamics

Analysis of bodies in accelerated motion, divided into kinematics (geometry of motion) and kinetics (forces causing motion).

  • LO-4: Analyse the geometry of motion of particles, rigid bodies and 2D linkages.
  • LO-5: Determine forces and energy associated in particles and rigid bodies in motion.
  • LO-6: Analyse natural vibrations of damped, single degree of freedom systems.
