Topic 01

Introduction & Material Properties

Everything you use every day—products, devices, components—is made of materials. Throughout history, the advancement of materials has gone hand-in-hand with the advancement of society. Entire eras of human development, such as the Stone Age, Bronze Age, and Iron Age, are named after the dominant materials of the time.

Today, there are over 60,000 materials available for engineers to select from. Because engineers are tasked with selecting the most appropriate materials to manufacture products, they must understand how different materials behave under different conditions.

This behavior is defined by a material's properties. A property is simply the response given by a material to a specific stimulus. Key property categories include:

  • Mechanical properties: The material's response to applied stress or force.
  • Electrical properties: The material's response to an electric field.
  • Magnetic properties: The material's response to a magnetic field.
  • Optical properties: The material's response to electromagnetic or light radiation.
