Bernoulli Equation

Definition(Bernoulli's Equation)

The differential equation
$$ \frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = f(x)y^n, $$
where $n$ is any real number, is called Bernoulli's equation.

  • When $n = 0$ and $n = 1$, this equation is linear. In these cases, it can be solved directly using the integrating factor method discussed in Section 4.
Method of Solving (Reduction to Linear Form)

For $n \neq 0, 1$, we use the substitution $v = y^{1-n}$ to reduces any equation of this form to a linear equation.

Step-by-step procedure:
  1. Divide the entire Bernoulli equation by $y^n$ to get:
    $$ y^{-n}\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y^{1-n} = f(x) $$
  2. Apply the substitution $v = y^{1-n}$. Differentiating both sides with respect to $x$ gives:
    $$ \frac{dv}{dx} = (1-n)y^{-n}\frac{dy}{dx} \implies y^{-n}\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{1}{1-n}\frac{dv}{dx} $$
  3. Substitute $v$ and $y^{-n}\frac{dy}{dx}$ back into the equation from Step 1:
    $$ \frac{1}{1-n}\frac{dv}{dx} + P(x)v = f(x) $$
  4. Multiply by $(1-n)$ to achieve standard linear form:
    $$ \frac{dv}{dx} + (1-n)P(x)v = (1-n)f(x) $$
    This is now a first-order linear ODE that can be solved for $v$ using an integrating factor.
  5. Once $v$ is found, substitute $y^{1-n}$ back in for $v$ to find the solution for $y$.
Worked Example: Solving a Bernoulli Equation

Solve the differential equation: $\frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{1}{x}y = xy^2$.

Step 1: Identify terms and divide by $y^n$.
Here, $P(x) = \frac{1}{x}$, $f(x) = x$, and $n = 2$.
Divide the equation by $y^2$:
$$ y^{-2}\frac{dy}{dx} + \frac{1}{x}y^{-1} = x $$

Step 2: Apply the substitution.
Let $v = y^{1-2} = y^{-1}$.
Then $\frac{dv}{dx} = -y^{-2}\frac{dy}{dx}$, which means $y^{-2}\frac{dy}{dx} = -\frac{dv}{dx}$.

Step 3: Substitute into the equation.
$$ -\frac{dv}{dx} + \frac{1}{x}v = x $$ $$ \frac{dv}{dx} - \frac{1}{x}v = -x $$
This is a linear equation where the new $P(x) = -\frac{1}{x}$ and new $Q(x) = -x$.

Step 4: Solve the linear equation using an Integrating Factor (IF).
$$ \text{IF} = e^{\int -\frac{1}{x} dx} = e^{-\ln|x|} = x^{-1} = \frac{1}{x} $$
Multiply the linear equation by $\frac{1}{x}$:
$$ \frac{1}{x}\frac{dv}{dx} - \frac{1}{x^2}v = -1 $$ $$ \frac{d}{dx}\left( \frac{v}{x} \right) = -1 $$
Integrate both sides:
$$ \frac{v}{x} = -x + C \implies v = Cx - x^2 $$

Step 5: Back-substitute to solve for $y$.
Replace $v$ with $y^{-1}$:
$$ \frac{1}{y} = Cx - x^2 \implies y = \frac{1}{Cx - x^2} $$
