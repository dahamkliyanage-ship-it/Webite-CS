About Our University

The University of Moratuwa (UoM) is widely recognized as the most acclaimed and prestigious technological university in Sri Lanka. Located in Katubedda, Moratuwa, it has a rich history originating from the Government Technical School founded in 1893, evolving over the decades into a premier centre of excellence in higher education.

UoM specializes strongly in engineering, architecture, information technology, business, and related scientific disciplines. The university is committed to fostering innovation, critical thinking, and cutting-edge research, producing world-class graduates who lead industries both locally and globally.

Key Highlights of the Faculty of Engineering:

  • Home to globally accredited undergraduate engineering degree programs under the Washington Accord.
  • State-of-the-art laboratory facilities, research centres, and workshops.
  • A vibrant academic environment supporting multidisciplinary learning, creativity, and technical excellence.
