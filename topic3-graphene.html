Topic 03

Graphene

Carbon has several distinct allotropes, including Graphite, Diamond, Buckminsterfullerene (1985), Carbon Nanotubes (1991), and finally Graphene (2004). Graphene isolation earned the Nobel Prize in 2010.

Crystal Structure

Graphene is a 2D crystal where carbon atoms are arranged in a flat, hexagonal network.

  • Unit Cell Shape: Rhombus.
  • Atoms per unit cell: 2 atoms.
  • Lattice Parameter: If the C-C bond length is d, the lattice parameter is calculated as 2d cos(30°).

Extraordinary Properties

Graphene boasts some of the most extreme properties ever recorded in materials science:

Property Approximate Value
Charge carrier mobility ~ 200,000 cm² / V·s
Thermal Conductivity ~ 5000 W / m·K
Transparency ~ 97.4%
Young's modulus ~ 1 TPa (1000 GPa)
Tensile strength ~ 1100 GPa
Band gap Zero
